ParkMate 🚗

Prototype Architecture Report
The Smart Urban Parking-Space Marketplace inspired by Airbnb's high-trust clarity, visual transparency, and seamless interaction design.
Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router) + Supabase (PostgreSQL 15) Language: Strict TypeScript & Zod Styling: Tailwind CSS v4 Date: October 2026

1. Executive Summary & Core Concept

In congested metropolitan areas, urban drivers lose an average of 15 to 20 minutes and significant fuel circling for parking, while private residential driveways, carports, and commercial bays sit vacant during standard business hours.

ParkMate solves this mismatch through a high-trust peer-to-peer and commercial marketplace. It enables homeowners to monetize unused workday parking while giving drivers guaranteed, clearance-verified, and fail-safe reservations near their destinations.

🚗 For Urban Drivers & Commuters
Guaranteed reservations with physical clearance verification, real-time map discovery, digital QR gate passes, 1-click time extensions, and automated booking recovery protection if a reserved spot is blocked.
🏡 For Homeowners & Space Hosts
Automated Smart Space Sharing monetization during idle workday hours (e.g., Mon–Fri 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM), dynamic pricing surge recommendations, draft listing wizards, and complete calendar control.

2. The 5 Core Architectural Differentiators

1. Vehicle Clearance & Compatibility Engine
Unlike generic directories, ParkMate checks vehicle heights, lengths, and widths against garage specs. Low-clearance bays display instant warnings (e.g., "Exceeds 6.8 ft ceiling limit") and safely disable booking to prevent vehicle damage.
2. Guaranteed Booking Recovery Failover
If a driver arrives and finds a space occupied, blocked, or the gate code inoperable, 1-click recovery scans nearby spaces within walking distance, checks vehicle compatibility, and reallocates an equal/upgraded spot at $0 extra charge (or issues 100% refund + credit).
3. FlexPark 1-Click Extensions
Drivers running behind schedule can extend reservations (+30m, +1h, +2h) on the fly. The server validates subsequent driver bookings in real-time, executing extensions instantly with zero overlapping conflicts.
4. Smart Space Sharing (Commute Monetization)
Residential hosts monetize driveway downtime during work hours (e.g., Mon–Fri 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM) while keeping the spot reserved for their own car in the evening. The engine analyzes calendar gaps and suggests 1-click publishable slots.
5. Explainable Demand Prediction & Dynamic Pricing AI
A deterministic heuristic model analyzing neighborhood density, rush-hour commuter surges, weekend patterns, and local events to forecast demand scores (0–100) with full transparency, accompanied by an interactive pricing simulator for hosts.

4. Backend Engines & Database Architecture

The core logic of ParkMate is decoupled into specialized domain engines located in src/lib/:

booking-engine.ts
State machine transitions (pending → confirmed → active → completed / cancelled / recovered), atomic conflict detection, and tamper-resistant price calculations.
compatibility-engine.ts
Multi-axis dimension matching (height, length, width, side clearance) with safety margins (4 inches vertical, 6 inches horizontal) across 6 vehicle classes.
recovery-engine.ts
Automated failover protocol. Discovers nearest compatible spaces within walking radius, calculates walking times, and waives price differences.
demand-engine.ts
Deterministic surge score engine (0–100) combining base neighborhood density, temporal rush hours (7–9 AM, 4–7 PM), and event flags.
slot-recommendation.ts
Gap analysis scanning owner recurring availability vs bookings to highlight unmonetized peak slots (e.g. Tuesday morning commute).
payment-engine.ts
Idempotent payment transaction orchestrator. Generates payment references, handles test card declines, and manages refund events.

5. Security, Concurrency & Data Integrity

  • PostgreSQL Exclusion Constraints: EXCLUDE USING gist (space_id WITH =, tstzrange(start_time, end_time) WITH &&) strictly prevents double bookings at the database engine level.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Supabase policies ensure hosts only edit their own spaces and drivers only access their own reservations.
  • Rate Limiting: In-memory sliding-window token bucket limiter protecting sensitive reservation and recovery routes.
  • MIME & File Sanitization: Image uploads checked against a strict whitelist (JPEG, PNG, WebP) with a 5MB limit and randomized storage paths.
  • Address & PIN Privacy: Exact addresses and gate PIN codes are stripped from search and listing endpoints until a booking is verified.
